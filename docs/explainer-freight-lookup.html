Freight lookup

A one-page tool for Sonny to check a customer address before quoting UPS shipping.

What she does

1. Type the address. After a short pause, real addresses appear under the box. Big buildings show "(12 units)" and open a list of apartments.
2. Pick one. The app checks it with Smarty, the address service, and fills in the full ZIP+4.
3. Read the cards. Residential or Commercial, rural surcharge yes or no, and the UPS zone for all six services.

Two ways to look up

Address
Needs Smarty. Gives Residential or Commercial and the exact address to copy.
ZIP only
No internet lookup at all. Gives zone and rural status from the UPS files.

Where the answers come from

UPS files
The zone chart for shipments from 088 and the June 1, 2025 area surcharge list. A script turns them into small data files the page reads.
Smarty
Address suggestions, the official address and the Residential or Commercial flag.

When UPS sends new files

Drop them in the data folder, run the build script, run the tests, push. The site updates itself. The README has the exact commands.

Why it is cheap to run

The page waits until typing pauses before asking Smarty, so one address costs a few lookups, not one per letter. Recent lookups are saved in the browser and reopen without any lookup.
